Data Methodology & Sources

Census Tract Poverty Data: The demographic shading is dynamically sourced from the 2024 American Community Survey (ACS) 5-Year Estimates. The ACS is an ongoing, nationwide survey conducted by the U.S. Census Bureau that provides highly detailed demographic, social, and economic data between decennial censuses.

Specifically, this map aggregates data from Table B17020: Poverty Status in the Past 12 Months by Sex by Age, calculating the total number of children under 18 living in households with incomes strictly below the official federal poverty measure.

Methodological Caveat: Federal poverty thresholds are uniform across the contiguous United States and do not adjust for regional cost-of-living disparities. Because the Greater Boston metropolitan area requires disproportionately high expenditures for housing and essentials, the official poverty measure likely underrepresents true economic hardship. Many families residing outside the heavily shaded tracts may still experience profound financial instability and qualify for programmatic support.
